## Cat meme poster (`cats/cats-wide.webp`, `cats/cats-tall.webp`, Others page header)

Stills captured from Queena Cat's compilation video "2025 這些貓界頂流你看過幾個🫨", then cut out with soft
masks and composited around an empty centre for the page title (shared colour grade, vignette and grain).
`cats-wide` is used on desktop and `cats-tall` on phones.
The original clips belong to their respective creators; these are **not** openly licensed and are shown,
like the concert posters, as fan references with a link to the source. Remove them if a rights holder asks.
[Source video](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=652929127632571)
