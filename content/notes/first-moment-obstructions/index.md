      text: |-
        These notes are based on Huy Tuan Pham's lecture series at *Innovations and Challenges in Extremal Combinatorics*, held at the Institute for Mathematical Sciences, National University of Singapore, in July 2026.

        I prepared and expanded the text from handwritten lecture notes taken by **Ying-Sien Wu**, together with photographs of the board. The notes follow the lectures' narrative, fill in sketched arguments, and include connecting remarks, figures, and bibliographic notes.

        The eight chapters are organised in three parts:

        - **The first moment method and thresholds:** first and second moments, covers, first moment obstructions, and the expectation threshold.
        - **The Kahn–Kalai theorem and spread:** the Park–Pham theorem, fractional covers and LP duality, spread measures, sunflowers, and smoother spread conditions.
        - **Beyond the complete graph:** robust thresholds for spanning structures and first moment obstructions for independent sets in sparse random Cayley graphs.

        The prerequisites are discrete probability and elementary graph theory. An appendix collects the probabilistic and analytic tools used in the text.
